Summarize the latest request, outcome, relevant paths, checks, and one next action. Keep it under 200 words. GSD owns phase artifacts in `.planning/`; the V2 plugin saves a small automatic handoff at idle and compaction.

When handing off a commit or release task, include the Conventional Commit type/release impact, resulting SHA (if committed), exact scope and pending compatibility/validation work; follow `docs/versioning.md`.
