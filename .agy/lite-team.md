# Lite teamwork

For bounded tasks with known ownership. Follow [shared contracts](howto.md) and the [canonical workflow](../docs/wiki/code/agent-workflow.md).

1. Lead fixes outcome, non-goals, acceptance, and dirty baseline, reusing existing authorization/plans.
2. One stack specialist implements (delegate independent docs/review early only when useful); lead owns integration and appoints one build/test owner in the integration tree.
3. Freeze after required edits and run affected checks; admit only minimal fixes for demonstrated in-scope blockers and defer independent improvements.
4. Deliver actual evidence and open gaps, then stop. Preserve unrelated work; commits/pushes require customer authorization.
