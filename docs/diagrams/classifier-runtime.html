Pinned Rust-BERT classification

Atlas requests an exact model pin from the private classifier service before live collection. The service loads a compatible Rust-BERT worker and pinned MNLI checkpoint. Atlas scores batches and applies its admission policy to the returned domain scores.

Transport readiness and model readiness are separate. A model pin failure appears in Jobs before article acquisition. The operator supplies the worker and model assets for the deployment architecture.
