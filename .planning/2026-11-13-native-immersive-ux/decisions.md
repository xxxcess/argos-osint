# Native Immersive UX Overhaul — Intake & Architecture Decisions

[Task plan](task_plan.md) · [Design blueprint](design.md) · [Findings](findings.md) · [Acceptance](acceptance.md)

1. **Supercede static-matte-only restriction in favor of layered adaptive surfaces**:
   - The November 12 contract in [native design system](../../docs/wiki/code/native-design-system.md) enforced flat opaque matte surfaces (`apps/fleetworks-desktop/src/material.rs`) with zero reflections or elevation.
   - Explicit user direction now calls for **liquid-metal button finishes with hover motion**, **faint glow on light UI elements**, and **mouse-hover shadow/highlight movement across liquid-metal surfaces**.
   - **Implemented decision**: Introduce a two-mode adaptive surface pipeline in `apps/fleetworks-desktop/src/material.rs` + `ui_components.rs` + `app.rs` using the `--locked` Rune stack:
     - **Active Liquid-Metal & Pointer-Lighting mode (default when `preferences.matte() == false`)**: `apply_liquid_metal` (`MetalFinish::{GoldCta, Gunmetal, CrimsonDanger, ObsidianPill}`), `luminous_card`, and `interactive_tile` combine multi-stop `El::linear_gradient` / `El::radial_gradient` / `border_gradient`, dual `box_shadow` drop + glow halos, `El::hover_motion` (`rune::Spring`) + `El::press_scale`, and `Live<[f32; 2]>` pointer-reactive glow (`pointer_offset`) + opposing cast shadows (`shadow_offset`) driven by `rune_kit::root(...).on_pointer_move_local(...)` (`tx.live_only()`).
     - **Accessible Matte / Reduced-Motion mode (`preferences.matte()` or `reduced_motion == true`)**: `cx.reduced_motion(true)` settles transitions, `material.rs` drops gradient overlays, pointer/shadow tracking (`pointer_ref = None`, `shadow_ref = None`), glow halos, and `press_scale`/`hover_motion`, rendering static high-contrast opaque fills with 1px borders and identical hit-bounds and `accessibility_json()` (`src/appearance_tests.rs`).

2. **Phase 2B architectural decision — retain `--locked` pure-Rune gradient/shadow pipeline**:
   - `rune`, `rune-kit`, and `rune-motion` are locked in `Cargo.lock` at `anomalyco/rune` rev `205b8d4`. Although `rune-fx` and `rune-light` exist in that upstream checkout, they are absent from `Cargo.lock`, and workspace policy mandates `python3 scripts/agent_cargo.py … --locked`.
   - **Decision**: Retain the `--locked` pure-Rune gradient, `box_shadow`, `Spring`, and `Live<[f32; 2]>` pointer-lighting pipeline (`material.rs` + `app.rs`) rather than adding unlocked `rune-fx`/`rune-light` crates to `Cargo.lock` under tight host disk headroom (~686 MiB free prior to cache hygiene). This keeps `Cargo.lock` frozen, avoids building new `wgpu`/`bytemuck` shader dependencies, and ensures identical rendering across `fleetworks-desktop` and headless `Runtime` fixtures without a separate compositor crate.

3. **Preserve functional allowlist, security/Keychain boundaries, and workstation scope**:
   - Overhauling layout, transitions, progressive disclosure, icons, checklists, toggles, and modal prompts **does not** re-introduce retired terminal/folder/Trash workflows, all-backend job fallbacks, raw Keychain secret exposure in UI state/confirmations, or unallowlisted tool-execution forms.
   - Every modal prompt (`Dialog` `modal_prompt` for `WorkstationInspector`, `selected_cycle_id`, `selected_job_id`, and `tool-api-key`) and inline toggle (`Switch` `inline_toggle_row` / `Checkbox` `checklist_row`) maps strictly to existing `State` intents and `Permission` guards (`src/app.rs`, `src/configuration.rs`, `src/submission.rs`, `src/pairing.rs`).

4. **Strict Brand Palette Anchoring with Elevated Contrast & Harmonized Status Diversity**:
   - Anchor all surfaces, chrome, and metallic ramps in `apps/fleetworks-desktop/src/theme.rs` to [fleetworks-mark.svg](../../assets/brand/fleetworks-mark.svg) (`BACKGROUND` `#060709`, `SURFACE` `#0B0D11`, `SURFACE_RAISED` `#12151C`, `SURFACE_ELEVATED` `#181C26`, `TEXT_PRIMARY` `#F7F3EA`, `TEXT_SECONDARY` `#DCD6C8`, `TEXT_MUTED` `#A8A296`, `ACCENT` `#C6A56E`, `ACCENT_PRESSED` `#C4A265`, `ACCENT_HOVER` `#E5C993`, `ACCENT_HIGHLIGHT` `#F2DFB6`, `ACCENT_DEEP` `#8E6E3B`, `SELECTION_SURFACE` `#322C22`, `BORDER_SUBTLE` `#3A3833`, `BORDER_CONTROL` `#948B7B`) plus gunmetal/glow stops (`METAL_DARK_*`, `LIGHT_SURFACE_GLOW` `#F7E8C9`, `GOLD_GLOW` `#D8BD8C`, `CAST_SHADOW` `#020304`).
   - Adopt the brand-harmonized semantic & entity palette aligned with [intel-theme.ts](../../packages/intel/src/shell/intel-theme.ts) via `StatusTone` (`Ok` `#5FD39A`, `Warn` `#E5B45B`, `Err` `#F06A8A`, `Info` `#5AB8F0`, `Violet` `#B48EFF`, `Cyan` `#21D9EE`, plus `ENTITY_PERSON` `#C08CF5`, `ENTITY_ORG` `#5AB8F0`, `ENTITY_COMPUTE` `#FFB347`, `ENTITY_CLUSTER` `#CAD9F0`) with tinted dark fills (`STATUS_*_BG` or 14% alpha) and crisp borders (`STATUS_*_BORDER` or 45% alpha).

5. **Planning, wiki & diagram synchronization**:
   - Phase 1 created planning artifacts under `.planning/2026-11-13-native-immersive-ux/` and synchronized `docs/wiki/code/ci.md` and `docs/wiki/code/native-verification.md` with `.github/workflows/`.

6. **Default home landing, connection-gated UI & reduced-sheen dim pointer lighting (`app.rs`, `views.rs`, `material.rs`)**:
   - **Decision**: Default landing state is always `Page::Workstation` (`setup_gateway: false`) on startup and disconnect, with `Setup Gateway` reachable via the offline `workstation-setup-gateway` CTA, toolbar `connection-editor` button, `gateway-status-header` (when disconnected), or sidebar `nav-setup`.
   - When `!state.connected()`, ALL gateway-dependent UI and routes are hidden: the sidebar shows ONLY `Workstation Status` (`nav-workstation`) and `Setup Gateway` (`nav-setup`), `views::page`/`toolbar`/`screen_key`/`navigate()`/`⌘1–6` guard against `Page::ALL`, `host-resources-card` and `readiness_cards::checks` are hidden on `Workstation Status`, `registration_status` prompts `"Connect to a gateway to register this workstation."`, and `scope_bar` renders `"Local Client"` (`local-client-scope`) + status pill. The bottom `sidebar-context-footer` card is removed.
   - Static metallic sheen (`top_edge`, `glow`, `hover-metal-sheen`, `liquid-specular`) is dialed down to a low-glare brushed finish, while `on_pointer_move_local` (`pointer_offset` `[-28..28, -19..19]`, `shadow_offset` `[-8..8, -7..7]`) treats the cursor as a very dim light source (`liquid-pointer-light` + `liquid-specular` + `pointer-glow` + opposing `cast-shadow`) across liquid-metal surfaces with exact `matte()` parity.

7. **Backend SurrealDB readiness/probe cancellation shielding (`crates/argos-osint-api/src/{lib.rs,workstation.rs,live.rs}`)**:
   - **Decision**: Rather than muting `surrealdb::engine::remote::ws` logs (`deploy/README.md` forbids muting it), run `storage_ready`, `workstation.rs` `DEADLINE` probes, and `live.rs` `3s` probes inside bounded detached tasks (`MAX_DETACHED_QUERIES = 32`, `run_shielded` / `run_shielded_timeout`) so outer HTTP/timeout cancellations drain in-flight WS replies cleanly instead of dropping `async_channel::Receiver` (`SendError`).
