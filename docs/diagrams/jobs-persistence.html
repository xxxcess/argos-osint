Jobs is the sole owner of operational SQLite state and the embedded RocksDB queue. Other services use authenticated HTTP. Domain services keep product data, business admission, publication fences and applied receipts in SurrealDB.

Commit the job mutation and enqueue intent together, then confirm the durable queue publication before marking the intent delivered. Replay unfinished intents after restart. Workers renew visibility and execution leases; acknowledge after durable settlement. A stable publication identity prevents another delivery for a replayed intent.

This figure shows the target. The SQLite and queue primitives precede the coordinated scheduler, telemetry and domain-handoff cutover. An HTTP lease check followed by a domain write does not preserve atomic publication: reconcile durable proposals and applied receipts before acknowledging conflicting cancellation or takeover.
