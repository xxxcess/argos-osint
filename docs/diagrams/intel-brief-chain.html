Intel Focus Brief stages and retained publication

Opening or reloading a Focus Brief admits a durable generation. A transaction saves its job, four tasks, captured inputs, and run visibility. Full article and Claims run independently; Inferences waits for both and Links waits for Inferences.

The background worker runs stages under leases. Publication checks the active generation so a superseded result cannot replace current evidence. A later failed stage preserves earlier successful publications.
