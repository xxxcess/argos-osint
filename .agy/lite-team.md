# Lite teamwork

Use for a bounded task with known ownership. Follow [shared contracts](howto.md) and the [canonical workflow](../docs/wiki/code/agent-workflow.md) for scope freeze, continuation and evidence.

1. The lead fixes outcome/non-goals/acceptance and the dirty baseline, reusing existing authorization and plans.
2. One stack specialist implements; delegate independent docs/review early only when useful. The lead owns integration and assigns one build/test owner in the integration tree.
3. Finish required changes, freeze and complete affected verification. Admit only minimal fixes for demonstrated in-scope blockers; defer independent improvements.
4. Deliver actual evidence and remaining gaps, then stop new work. Preserve unrelated changes and unresolved evidence; commits/pushes follow customer authorization.
