Venture startup and ownership

Venture initializes the workspace, selects a storage mode, reconciles its owned services, and checks storage-backed API readiness before returning or attaching a terminal.

Local Net connects to running SurrealDB and RabbitMQ endpoints. Local Process starts both on the host and preserves their data on stop. Use --atlas when the workspace needs Atlas collection. Stop the owned stack before changing its storage mode or Atlas overlay.
