Native review artifacts and trusted release

Native candidates always select both Macs from one exact pushed SHA. The configured workflow produces unsigned review ZIPs and validates the downloaded artifact bytes. Installed-app acceptance and trusted release promotion remain separate recorded work.

The candidate workflow accepts publish=none and has no signing or release-write credentials. Its download check verifies architecture, libraries, identity, source and checksums without installing or launching the app. Generated fixture evidence does not establish actual GUI use.

Review links are the actual per-architecture Actions artifact/evidence URLs, retained for fourteen days. Durable release assets require a separate trusted promotion after matching-hardware, minimum-OS and signing/notarization evidence; the diagram does not imply that promotion is automated or complete.
